One courtyard · twelve months

The house holds still. The year does not.

A single continuous shot from one point. Scroll, and twelve months pass through it.

Scroll

June – August · longest light

The garden is at full height

The lawn is mown in stripes and keeps its colour until evening. The canopy has closed over, so the terrace stays in shade for most of the day. The pool is open and the water lies flat.

September – November · leaf fall

The canopy lets go

One tree at the edge turns first and the rest follow over a few weeks. Leaves land on the water faster than anyone skims them off, and gold collects across the lawn. The grass is still green, but it is a duller green than in July.

December – February · snow cover

Snow takes away the edges

The line between lawn and terrace disappears, and so does the one between path and border. The pool is covered and reads as a rectangle in a slightly different white. Only the house keeps its shape.

March – May · blossom

Colour arrives before the leaves

Snow goes in patches: the lawn darkens first, then the terrace clears. Blossom opens on bare branches, pink before anything is green, and tulips come up along the border in pink and orange. These weeks read brighter than July.
